About Rohan

A cyber expert who
speaks kids' language.

Growing up navigating an increasingly complex digital world, I know firsthand what it means to feel lost online. Today, I channel that experience — and my expertise in cybersecurity and internet governance — into empowering the next generation.

Through my work in internet governance and cybersecurity — including hands-on experience investigating phishing campaigns, scams, and online threats — I've seen the real harm that digital dangers cause. Through Dexteris, I bring that front-line knowledge directly into classrooms, schools, and community programs.

My workshops are practical, engaging, and age-appropriate — designed to build genuine confidence online, not just fear of the internet.

What I Offer

Workshops that
actually stick.

Each session is interactive, curriculum-aligned, and tailored to the age group. No boring lectures — just real skills, real scenarios, and real conversations.

01
Cyber Safety Foundations
An introduction to online threats, password security, and safe browsing habits. Perfect for primary school students building their digital footprint for the first time.
Years 3–6
02
Social Media & Privacy
Understanding data privacy, what you share online, cyberbullying, and how to protect your identity. Hands-on scenarios that mirror real social media dilemmas.
Years 7–10
03
Scams, Phishing & Fraud
Recognising scams, phishing emails, fake websites, and social engineering. Based on real-world threat intelligence from Australia's national domain registry.
Years 9–12
04
AI, Deepfakes & Digital Trust
Navigating AI-generated content, disinformation, and how to critically evaluate what you see online. Timely, relevant, and grounded in current research.
Years 9–12
05
Parent & Educator Briefings
A dedicated session for parents, teachers, and school staff covering current digital threats, how to talk to kids about online safety, and school-wide strategies.
Adults
06
School Incursion Packages
Full-day tailored programs combining multiple workshops, assemblies, and teacher PD. Designed for schools wanting a comprehensive whole-of-community approach.

About Rohan

A cybersecurity expert who speaks kids' language.

Growing up navigating an increasingly complex digital world, I know firsthand what it means to feel lost online. Today I channel that experience — and my expertise in cybersecurity and internet governance — into empowering the next generation.

My workshops are practical, engaging, and age-appropriate — designed to build genuine confidence online, not just fear of the internet.

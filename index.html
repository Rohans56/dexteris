About Rohan

A bit about who I am.

I'm Rohan — a Melbourne-based cybersecurity expert, internet governance advocate, and founder of Dexteris. I started this because I believe every young Australian deserves to feel confident and safe online.

My workshops aren't lectures. They're real conversations, real scenarios, and real skills — delivered in a way that actually resonates with young people. Because I've been that young person too.

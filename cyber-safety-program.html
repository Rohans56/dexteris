Workshop Topics

What we cover.

01

Cyber Safety Foundations

Years 3–6 Primary

An introduction to staying safe online for students who are building their digital footprint for the first time. Practical, age-appropriate, and engaging — not a lecture.

  • Creating and protecting strong passwords
  • Safe browsing habits and recognising unsafe websites
  • What personal information is and why we protect it
  • Who to talk to when something feels wrong online
  • Understanding the difference between public and private
02

Social Media & Privacy

Years 7–10 Middle School

Real social media dilemmas, not hypotheticals. Students work through scenarios that mirror their actual online experiences — from Instagram and TikTok to gaming and Discord.

  • What you share online and what it reveals about you
  • Privacy settings across major platforms
  • Understanding cyberbullying and how to respond
  • Digital identity — what your online presence says about you
  • Recognising grooming tactics and online manipulation
  • Australia's under-16 social media laws — what they mean for you
03

Scams, Phishing & Fraud

Years 9–12 Senior

Based on real Australian threat intelligence — the same phishing campaigns and scam tactics targeting Australians right now. Students learn to spot them before they become victims.

  • How phishing emails and SMS scams actually work
  • Spotting fake websites and spoofed domains (e.g. mygovt-australia.com)
  • Bank impersonation and government scams targeting young Australians
  • Social engineering — how scammers build trust before striking
  • What to do if you've been scammed — reporting and recovery
  • Interactive: spot the scam in real-world Australian examples
04

AI, Deepfakes & Digital Trust

Years 9–12 Senior New

The most timely topic in cyber safety right now. Students learn how AI-generated content is being used to manipulate, deceive and harm — and how to critically evaluate what they see online.

  • How deepfakes are created and why they're dangerous
  • AI-generated scam messages — why they're so convincing
  • Voice cloning fraud and what it sounds like
  • Identifying AI-generated misinformation and fake news
  • Sextortion using AI-generated images — what to do
  • Building a critical eye: how to verify before you trust
05

Online Identity & Digital Reputation

Years 7–12 Middle & Senior

What students post today can follow them for years. This session helps students understand their digital footprint and take control of how they present themselves online.

  • What is a digital footprint — and who can see yours?
  • How employers, universities and strangers search for you online
  • Managing your online reputation proactively
  • Data brokers — who has your information and what they do with it
  • Understanding privacy laws and your rights as a young person
06

Cyber Safety Leadership Program

Years 9–12 Leadership Extended

Available as part of the two-term package. Students don't just learn cyber safety — they become advocates for it. This program trains senior students to lead cyber safety conversations with their peers and younger students.

  • Understanding cyber threats at a deeper, strategic level
  • How to explain digital safety concepts to younger students
  • Running a peer-led cyber safety session
  • Building a school-wide culture of digital responsibility
  • Certificate of completion for student leaders
07

School Incursion Packages

All Year Levels Whole School

A full-day tailored program that brings multiple sessions, assemblies, and teacher PD together for a whole-of-community approach to cyber safety.

  • Morning session: Primary or middle school workshop
  • Midday: Senior secondary session on advanced threats
  • Assembly slot: School-wide awareness presentation
  • Afternoon: Teacher professional development briefing
  • Take-home resources for students and families
